Task
So, yeah, stereotypes are not nice, but today we're going to make fun of them.

1. Pick two types of people you can find in the university. They could be people who belong to urban groups or schools of the institution (e.g. students from the School of Engineering; students that belong to the School of Human Sciences).

2. Check the images that have been attached to this post and read the descriptions of those types of people.

3. Taking into account the images you looked at and the groups you chose, create two images (one per group) with the description of those groups. Generate a visual image based on the usual physical look of those people, the clothes they wear, the accessories they have, and add descriptions. The descriptions should be about what they wear (clothes or accessories), their physical appearance, and also about behaviors.
