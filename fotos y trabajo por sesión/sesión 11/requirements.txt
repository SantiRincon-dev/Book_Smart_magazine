ask November 12th (Photo + Stolen painting)
Task 1

1. Your mission is to take several photos around campus. You can spend about 45 minutes doing that and then come back to the classroom.

2. Look at all the photos you took. Choose your 8 favorite photos, which must be included in a photo gallery in the magazine.

Task 2

You are going to write a report on the theft of a famous painting. Follow these steps:

1. Use an AI image generator to create a painting. To do this, decide what you want your painting to look like (theme, style, etc.). Please, write the prompt in English.

2. Give a name to the painting and decide who painted it (it could be a fictional painter or one person from the group).

3. Your painting is the one that has been stolen. Thus, complete the information in the PDF that has been attached to create the news report on the crime.
