Task 1: Create 4 top 5s related to movies or series.

1. Discuss which categories you are going to use for your top 5s (E.g. Top 5 best movies ever, best series, best actors, best soundtracks, best superhero movies, best book adaptations, best 2020s series, greatest villains ever, etc.)

2. After choosing your 4 categories, create a top 5 for each one. This implies brainstorming ideas and making agreements.

3. Write a short introduction for the article. Do NOT write individual descriptions for each top 5, just a general introduction to the whole article.

4. Use visuals for each top 5.
