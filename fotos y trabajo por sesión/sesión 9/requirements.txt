Tasks: Write two haikus and a fifty-word story

Task 1 - Haikus
1. Read the examples of haikus and 50-word story in the PDF attached to this post.
2. Choose a theme for the haikus and begin writing them.

Task 2 - 50-word story
1. Choose a title for your 50-word story from the list and write the story based on it.
2. Include an inversion in your narration.

Visuals
Include an image for each haiku and for the story, something that represents what you are writing about.
