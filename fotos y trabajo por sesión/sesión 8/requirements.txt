Task 1 - Heart Songs
1. You are going to create a section in your magazine about you and music.

2. Each one of you is going to think of a song in English that you like because of the lyrics. Maybe these lyrics have a special connection with you and a part of your life.

3. Each of you is going to choose one part of the lyrics of that song. It could be a verse, a chorus, or a line. Then, tell your group mates why you love this part, how it makes you feel, or how it is connected with you. Then, put that into words. Copy and paste the lyrics (include the name of the song and the artist) and write your thoughts on the song.

Task 2 - My magazine recommends
1. Create a playlist on any music streaming service. Name the playlist like this:
 ________ (your magazine name) Recommends.

2. Include the previous songs in the playlist, plus a couple of songs in English that you love. Try to include around 30 songs that you guys really enjoy listening to.

3. List the songs and the corresponding artists. 

E.g.
- Flowers by Miley Cyrus
- Bad Guy by Billie Eilish

4. Create a cover for your list and save it into the Playlist.

5. Write an introduction to the article and include the link to your playlist.
