Task: writing a short story

This week, you guys will be writing a short story in your project groups. To do so, follow these instructions:

1. Pick up your cards. The yellow cards correspond to characters, and the orange ones have to do with situations. The two yellow cards can describe two different characters or both can be used to create just one.

2. Plan and write a story based on those characters and the situations from the orange cards. You can include other elements and characters, of course (250 words approx.).

3. Be careful with the narrative tense you use. You can narrate in the present or past tense, but don't go switching from one to the other as the coherence is affected.

4. Include two inversions in the story to create a dramatic effect.

5. When you finish your story, choose a title, and create an image for visual support
