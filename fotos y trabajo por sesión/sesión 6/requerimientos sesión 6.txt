Task 2 - Overrated / Underrated
1. Talk about a movie/series/actor you consider is overrated even though many people praise it.
2. On the other hand, talk about a movie/series/actor you think is underrated.
3. Write a short paragraph (one per choice / 100 words each) where you describe an overrated movie/series/actor and then an underrated one. 
If your choice is a movie or series: What genre is the movie/series? What is the movie/series about? Who stars the movie/series? Why are they overrated/underrated?
If your choice is an actor: who is he/she? What famous roles have they had? Why do you think he/she is an overrated/underrated actor? 
4. Add visual aids.
