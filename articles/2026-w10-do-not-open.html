Politics Week 10

Do Not Open

A Short Story

A short story in two parts, made for Session 10.

Editorial Board November 6, 2026

The cards

  • Character with a mysterious past:

    Bruce Johnson

  • Character as a cashier at a dollar store:

    Samantha Anderson

The story

A few months ago, Bruce Johnson decided to start over again in Seattle trying to leave behind his past.

Recently, he has fallen in love with a wonderful woman, Samantha, who has accepted him for who he is for the first time in his life. On the other hand, Samantha Anderson belongs to a humble family, that’s why she works hard as a cashier at a dollar store while she is finishing her engineering degree at the University Of Washington.

As the relationship progresses, Samantha has noticed that Bruce is very discreet when he talks about his past. As a result, she starts to feel more suspicious every time he avoids a conversation. Everything changes when she unexpectedly receives an enigmatic envelope with a warning outside.

Only by opening the letter would the truth be revealed, but if you want to keep your life as always, you must never open it

Samantha has always been a curious woman. That is why she decides to open the envelope despite her worries. Only in her nightmares had she dreamed the truth. After that, she finds out that her boyfriend is a serial killer. All of their relationship has been a lie, at least to her. She doesn’t want to believe the envelope contains. So she stays awake all night and she walks along the river board of the city and she smokes a complete pack of cigarettes.

The next day, Samantha makes the decision to run away. But her boyfriend arrives home in the morning and she already knows that there is no place to hide anymore.
